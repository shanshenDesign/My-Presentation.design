01 · Context

The city is
the site.

Design problem

Urban residential plots are narrow, noisy, and often overshadowed by neighboring buildings. The house must turn these constraints into advantages: controlled views, layered privacy, and daylight pulled deep into the plan.

02 · Strategy

Three moves
shape the house

Move 01

Stack
the program

Public living spaces sit at street level; bedrooms retreat upward. A vertical core keeps circulation compact and frees the plan for light and air.

Move 02

Carve
the void

A rear courtyard and a narrow light well cut through the volume, bringing sky and ventilation into the deepest rooms.

Move 03

Filter
the street

A perforated metal screen and planted terrace buffer noise while preserving a visible, welcoming connection to the neighborhood.
